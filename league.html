League

----
Pinch to zoom · ⚔️ pulsing stations are next to your land on an MRT line — attack those. ⭐ white-ring interchanges defend with 1.25× chips, 🧱 walls add +10% each. You duel the owner's 👻 ghost (it plays for them while they're away). House bots hold unclaimed towns.

🎯 Goal

Own the most MRT stations. Each season lasts one calendar month: whoever holds the most stations at month-end wins a permanent 🏆, then the map resets and everyone gets a fresh home station. Weekly titles (⚔️ Conqueror, 🛡 Wall) run alongside.

⚔️ Attacking

Tap a pulsing ⚔️ station — it must be next to one of yours on an MRT line (if you own none, attack anywhere). You duel the owner's 👻 ghost, which plays for them while they're away. If they're online, you can send a ⚡ live challenge instead.

A duel = repeated 5-round games at a fixed bet every game; banker alternates (defender banks first). First side that can't cover the bet loses — or after 30 games, whoever has more chips wins. Win and the station is yours.

The attack screen shows your win chance before you go. Roughly: your chips ÷ (your chips + defender's chips).

⚡ Attack energy: max 5. Every attack (win or lose) uses 1. You get +1 back every 5 hours, and it refills to full at midnight (SG time). Out of ⚡? An extra attack costs 1× bet in coins.

🐣 Underdog boost: attacking a friend who holds more stations than you gives you +5% chips per station they lead by (max +30%).

🛡 Defending

You don't need to be online — your ghost defends every station automatically. Defenders start with extra chips:

  • ⭐ Interchange (2+ lines): ×1.25
  • 🧱 Each wall level: +10% (max Lv3)

Lose a station and its wall drops one level for the new owner.

🪙 Coins

  • Each station makes 1× bet a day; ⭐ interchanges 2.5×. Storage holds 2 days — collect at least every other day.
  • 🎁 Daily bonus on your first Collect each day: 2× bet, rising 1× each day in a row, up to 5× bet. Miss a day and the streak resets.
  • 🧱 Walls (your stations): Lv1/2/3 cost 1×/2×/4× bet.
  • ⚔️ Army: 1 coin = 1 extra chip on your next attack, up to +1 bankroll.
  • 💰 Bounty: capture a tougher station and earn coins = the defender's extra chips.
  • 🏴‍☠️ Loot: duel winner takes 20% of the loser's coins.

📋 Daily mission

One small goal a day (win an attack, make 3 attacks, or build a wall). Finish it for 2× bet in coins, paid automatically.

🚇 Line Tycoon

Own every station on a whole MRT line to earn its Tycoon title.

🃏 Friends' tables

Playing a normal online table together? The host enters league code in room settings and your results count on the Ranks tab.
